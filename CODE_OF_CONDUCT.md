# Code of Conduct

Ritru expects professional, respectful, direct, and evidence-based collaboration.

Contributors must:

- challenge ideas without attacking people;
- communicate risks, uncertainty, and mistakes honestly;
- protect customer, employee, and company information;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, intimidation, and retaliation;
- give useful review feedback and accept accountable technical disagreement;
- prioritise safety and customer impact over personal ownership of an idea.

Concerns should be raised privately with Ritru founding leadership. Reports will be handled with appropriate confidentiality, fairness, and urgency.
