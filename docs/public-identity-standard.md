# Public Identity Standard

## Principle

Public Ritru repositories represent the company, not an individual employee or founder.

## Requirements

- Public documentation must use company roles or organisation teams instead of personal names or individual account handles.
- `CODEOWNERS` should reference stable Ritru organisation teams. Do not publish individual ownership as a substitute for establishing team ownership.
- Personal employment, client, contact, or biographical information must not be included unless explicitly approved for a public company purpose.
- Customer identities, private employers, and unrelated professional affiliations must never appear in reusable Ritru repositories.
- Repository history and GitHub authorship metadata are platform records; contributors should use an approved company or dedicated contribution identity where separation is required.

## Transitional state

Until Ritru organisation teams are created, ownership remains documented by company role in repository documentation and enforced through organisation administration. Add team-based `CODEOWNERS` only after the relevant team exists and has the correct membership and permissions.
